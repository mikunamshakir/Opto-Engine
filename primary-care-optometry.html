Foundations of Eye Care

Primary care optometry is the first line of defense for eye health, involving routine exams, diagnosis of common conditions, and prescription of corrective lenses.

Our resources provide in-depth information on refractive errors, common eye diseases, and the latest diagnostic tools. We aim to equip optometrists with current knowledge for excellent patient care.

Ocular & Medical Optometry

Advanced diagnosis and management of complex eye diseases and systemic conditions affecting vision.

Specialized Care for Ocular Health

Ocular and medical optometry focuses on the diagnosis, treatment, and co-management of various eye diseases and vision conditions, often collaborating with other medical specialists.

Our resources provide detailed protocols for managing glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, and other serious conditions. We emphasize evidence-based practices and interdisciplinary approaches to care.

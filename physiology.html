How Vision Works: From Light to Perception

Physiology delves into the mechanisms of life. In vision, this means understanding how light energy is converted into electrical signals and interpreted by the brain.

Our resources cover everything from the photochemical reactions in the retina to the complex neural pathways of the visual cortex. Gain insights into visual acuity, color perception, depth perception, and eye movements.

Key Physiological Processes

Phototransduction

The conversion of light into electrical signals by photoreceptor cells.

Accommodation

The eye's ability to change optical power to maintain a clear image at varying distances.

Binocular Vision

How two eyes work together to create a single, three-dimensional perception of the world.

Color Perception

The mechanisms by which the eye and brain differentiate between various wavelengths of light.

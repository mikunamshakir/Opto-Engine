Key landmarks
  • Sclera – outer fibrous coat
  • Cornea – clear anterior window
  • Iris – regulates light entry
  • Lens – focusing element
  • Ciliary body – aqueous & accommodation
  • Retina – neurosensory layer
  • Choroid – vascular supply
  • Optic disc – nerve exit (blind spot)
  • Macula / Fovea – high-acuity center

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Learn how Opto Engine collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective Date: April 4, 2026
Opto Engine is committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about our practices. This policy explains your rights and our obligations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all users. The types of information we gather include:

  • Personal Information: When you register, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, institution/organization, and professional credentials.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information about your interaction with our website, including IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, time spent, referring URLs, and search queries (via Google Programmable Search Engine).
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies: We use cookies to enhance user experience, analyze trends, and personalize content. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
  • Analytics Data: We utilize Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics to understand traffic patterns and improve our services. These tools may collect data such as device information and geographic location.

2. How We Use Your Information

Opto Engine uses collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our website and research database.
  • To improve, personalize, and expand our educational and research resources.
  • To communicate with you — including sending newsletters, research updates, responses to inquiries, and service announcements.
  • To analyze usage trends and enhance user experience (anonymized or aggregated data).
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliance for EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using personal information depends on the specific context:

  • Consent: When you subscribe to our newsletter or accept cookies.
  • Contractual necessity: To provide services you requested (e.g., account registration).
  • Legitimate interests: For improving our platform, preventing fraud, and ensuring network security.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to remember preferences, analyze site traffic, and enable essential functions. Below is a summary:

Cookie TypePurposeDuration
Essential CookiesEnable core functionality (navigation, login)Session / Persistent
Analytics Cookies (Google, Vercel)Collect anonymous traffic dataUp to 2 years
Preference CookiesRemember user settings (e.g., search preferences)1 year

You can manage or delete cookies via your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

Opto Engine integrates with trusted third-party services to enhance functionality:

  • Google Programmable Search: Provides site search functionality. Google may collect search queries and IP addresses per its Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics: Tracks user interactions. You can opt-out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Vercel Web Analytics: Collects anonymized performance data. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.
  • Unsplash & Font Awesome: Used for imagery and icons; they may collect limited usage data as described in their respective policies.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption (SSL/TLS), access controls, and regular vulnerability assessments, to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. Account information is kept until you request deletion or after a period of inactivity (2 years).

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction: Limit how we use your data.
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Opt-out of Marketing: Unsubscribe from newsletters via the "unsubscribe" link in any email.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@optoengine.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Opto Engine is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

10. International Data Transfers

Our servers are located in the United States. If you access Opto Engine from outside the US, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US where our data centers operate. By using our service, you consent to this transfer. For EEA users, we ensure appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Effective Date" at the top indicates when the policy was last revised. Material changes will be notified via a prominent notice on our website or via email. Your continued use of Opto Engine after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

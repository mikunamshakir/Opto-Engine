Low Vision Optometry

Empowering individuals with visual impairments through rehabilitation, assistive technologies, and specialized care.

Restoring Functional Vision

Low vision refers to visual impairment that cannot be fully corrected with glasses, contacts, or medical treatment — affecting over 250 million people worldwide.

Our low vision optometry resources focus on maximizing remaining vision, prescribing optical and electronic aids, and providing rehabilitation strategies to improve quality of life and independence.

"Low vision care is not just about magnification — it's about helping patients rediscover their abilities."

Common Causes & Clinical Approaches

Age-Related Macular Degeneration

Central vision loss. Management: magnification, scotoma training.

Diabetic Retinopathy

Fluctuating vision, floaters. Management: contrast enhancement, lighting.

Glaucoma

Peripheral field loss. Management: prisms, field expansion devices.

Retinitis Pigmentosa

Tunnel vision, night blindness. Management: mobility training, lighting.
